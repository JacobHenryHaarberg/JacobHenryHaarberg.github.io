50W High-Voltage Tube Amplifier

Analog Hardware

For this project, I designed and built a 50W tube amplifier including the High-Voltage Power Supply, low noise preamp, phase invertsion, and A/B Power output stage. I ended up spending a really conciderable amount of time on this project.
I couldnt have done any of this without these reasources:

Valve Amplifiers by Morgan Jones; taught me everything about tube amplifier analysis and operation. I cant understate how much this textbook helped me out with this project.

Uncle Doug on youtube; has amazing lectures and information videos all things tube amplifiers.

Tech: LTspice, Fixed Biasing, Ground Loop Elimination, Impedance Matching
